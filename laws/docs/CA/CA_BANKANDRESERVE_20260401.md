## Preamble

The Hudson Republic recognizes that sound money is the foundation of individual liberty, economic sovereignty, and resistance to centralized financial control or foreign influence. All monetary obligations must be strictly backed by verifiable physical bullion to prevent fractional-reserve abuse, inflation, and fiduciary betrayal. Reserves shall be transparent, mathematically simple (expressed in binary bitshift equivalents), and subject to rigorous, chemistry-verified audits.

This Act operates in close coordination with the **[Legal Tender Act](./CA_LEGALTENDER_20260401.md)**, the **[Banking Insurance Standards Act](../LA/LA_BANKINSURANCESTANDARDS_20260525.md)**, and the **[Banking Administration Schedule](../SCH/SCH_BANKADMIN_20260412.md)**. Together they establish uniform reserve requirements, insurance safeguards, liquidation protocols, allocation rules, and enforcement mechanisms.

## Section 1 — Monetary Reserves and Backing

**1.1** All Hudson Ledger obligations—including loans, contracts, trusts, deposits, and other liabilities—shall be fully backed by physical bullion held in FHI-insured vaults. This applies to both the National Holdings Vault and private institutions.  
**1.2** Reserve ratios shall be expressed and enforced exclusively in powers of two (**binary bitshift equivalents**) for mathematical transparency and audit simplicity.  
**1.3** The reserve ratio is defined as the proportion of vaulted physical bullion relative to total outstanding ledger obligations.

---

## Section 2 — Reserve Ratio Limits (Bitshift Enforcement)

Permitted reserve ratios are restricted to the following discrete bitshift steps; intermediate values are strictly prohibited:

| Bitshift | Fractional Equivalent | Percentage | Status                  |
|----------|-----------------------|------------|-------------------------|
| Shift of 4 | 1/16                | 6.25%      | **Minimum Required Ratio** |
| Shift of 3 | 1/8                 | 12.5%      | Permitted               |
| Shift of 2 | 1/4                 | 25.0%      | **Maximum Allowable Ratio** |

## Section 3 — Audit and Enforcement

**3.1 Quarterly Audits**: Compliance verified every quarter by an independent FHI- and MTI-certified inspector.  
**3.2 Chemistry Verification**: Audits prioritize chemical purity assay (via XRF or equivalent) over weight.  
**3.3 Annual Cycle Review**: The December 31 audit requires full public disclosure of reserve holdings, volumes, and compliance status.  
**3.4 Non-Compliance**: If reserves fall outside bitshift limits, the institution immediately loses FHI coverage.  
**3.5 Fraud Oversight**: Inside-job fraud results in permanent FHI license revocation. External fraud is referred to the Hudson Intelligence Service (HIS).

---

## Section 4 — Vault-to-Ledger Liquidation Protocol

**4.1** Only bullion meeting **[Legal Tender Act](./CA_LEGALTENDER_20260401.md)** standards may be held in FHI vaults.  
**4.2** High-purity bars (≥99.5%) may be transferred 1-to-1 between FHI institutions.  
**4.3 Liquidation**: Institutions or merchants may sell bars (≥99.5%) for bullion or Ledger credits using XRF-verified data. No sale shall reduce backing below the 1/16 minimum.  
**4.4 Net Settlement**: Physical movement of bullion is optional if net-settled quarterly via Ledger units.  
**4.5 Full Backing**: All Ledger obligations must be backed at the required ratio.  
**4.6 Electronic Exemption**: Purely electronic Ledger transactions (no physical exchange) are exempt from Merchant Transaction Insurance (MTI).

---

## Section 5 — Allocation of Mined Reserves

**5.1 Annual Allocation**: 20–30% of all bullion mined within the Republic (e.g., Ring of Fire, Canadian Shield) must be directed to financial reserves.  
**5.2 Financial Reserve Split**:  
* **50%**: Held as high-purity bars (≥99.5%) in vaults for stability.  
* **50%**: Minted into Hudson beaver bullion (95% alloy) for circulation.

---

## Section 6 — Institutional Uniformity

**6.1** Private banks and the National Holdings Vault are subject to identical ratios, calculations, and audit protocols.  
**6.2** No private institution may adopt alternative backing methods or exemptions.

---

## Section 7 — Enforcement and Penalties

**7.1** Breaches of ratios, allocation, or audits constitute a material breach of fiduciary duty.  
**7.2 Penalties**:  
* Immediate suspension of lending privileges.  
* Absolute personal liability for responsible officers.  
* Criminal prosecution under the **Sequential Mandate**.
