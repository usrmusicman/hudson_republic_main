## Preamble

This Act establishes the detailed administrative, procedural, and operational rules for territorial changes, claims processing, secession, accession, and Satellite State relationships within the framework defined by the **[Territorial Provisioning and Domain Administration Act](./CA_TERRITORIALPROVISION_20260401.md)**.

---

## Section 1 — Modification of the Union: Onboarding and Secession

**1.1 Exit (Secession)**  
- Secession may only occur at the Array level (exactly twelve contiguous Ridings).  
- Each Riding in the seceding Array must approve by **Overwhelming Majority** referendum.  
- The Senate must grant final approval by **Overwhelming Majority** verdict across three readings.

**1.2 Standard Exit Fee**  
Upon secession, the departing Array shall immediately pay an Exit Fee to the Hudson Republic. This fee equals the departing proportion of total Ridings (number of departing Ridings ÷ total Ridings prior to secession) multiplied by a factor between 0.5 and 1.0, applied to the Array’s proportional share of the Republic’s public debt. The exact multiplier shall be determined by **Overwhelming Majority** Senate verdict.

**1.3 Bondage Exit (Exceptional Relief)**  
In extraordinary cases, a seceding Array may petition the Supreme Court for a **Bondage Exit**. If the Supreme Court delivers a **unanimous verdict** that the Republic has imposed state-sanctioned bondage upon the seceding territory, all public debt obligations shall be waived. In such cases, only the standard secession application fee of **fifty (50) troy ounces of gold** shall be payable.  

It is strongly recommended that a downgrade to Satellite State status be considered first as part of the referendum question, though full secession remains permissible.

**1.4 Joining (Accession as Ridings)**  
- Applying territories must form a complete Array (minimum 360,000 km², maximum 600,000 km²).  
- Applicants must undergo full security and integrity screening by the Hudson Intelligence Service.  
- Joining requires **Overwhelming Majority** approval by referendum in the applying territory and **Overwhelming Majority** Senate verdict.  
- The National Representative holds veto power.

**1.5 Claim Application Window and Processing Period**  
Once a territorial claim for secession or accession is formally submitted, all other competing or related claims must be filed within **ninety (90) days** of the first claimant.  

**1.6 Election Protection Rule**  
No territorial claim period (secession, accession, or related claims) may commence or remain active within **six (6) months** before or after a scheduled general election. This restriction exists to prevent election interference and maintain the integrity of the democratic process.

Following the closure of the 90-day filing window, the Republic shall have an additional **ninety (90) days** to process and adjudicate all submitted claims.  

After the full 180-day period has elapsed, no further claims related to that redraw cycle may be submitted. The next claim application window shall not open until **ten (10) years** from the closing date of the previous claim period.

## Section 2 — Satellite States (Limited Federation)

**2.1 Purpose and Status**  
Satellite States maintain a loose affiliation with the Hudson Republic. They receive trade access within the Hudson Economic Zone, military protection from the Hudson United Guard (HUG), and passport priority, but hold no voting rights in the Senate.

**2.2 Application Process**  
- An application fee of **fifty (50) troy ounces of gold** (paid exclusively from public funds) is required to cover legal, forensic audit, and administrative costs.  
- The applying region must not exceed **30,000 km²**.  
- Approval requires signatures from two Riding Representatives and final sign-off by the National Representative.  
- Satellite State applications may be submitted **at any time**.

**2.3 Mutual Exclusivity Rule**  
A region may apply for Satellite State status or for full adoption as Ridings, but not both simultaneously.

---

## Section 3 — Adoption of Satellite States as Ridings

**3.1 Application Process**  
- An application fee of **five hundred (500) troy ounces of gold** (paid from public funds) is required.  
- Adoption requires a Legislative Challenge (LC) and a referendum in the affected Senatorial block achieving the **Overwhelming Majority** standard, followed by a Senate verdict.  
- The National Representative may veto or approve the adoption. Approved adoptions enter a thirty (30)-year probationary period.

**3.2 Mutual Exclusivity Rule**  
A region that applies for one category and is rejected, rescinds, or is revoked must wait the full prescribed waiting period before applying for the other category.

## Section 4 — Claims Assessment Process

**4.1 General Principles**  
All applications for Satellite State status, adoption as Ridings, formal joining, or secession shall undergo a formal Claims Assessment Process conducted by the Senate and the National Representative. Applications are processed first-come, first-served.

**4.2 Weighted Decision Factors**  
The assessment shall be based on the following weighted criteria:

- **First-Come, First-Served Priority (10–15%)**: Earlier applications receive greater consideration in cases of overlapping land claims.  
- **Claim to Territory (70–75%)**: The assessment shall determine who holds the most recent valid title to the claimed territory, whether public or private.  
  - Valid claims require formal documentation (mortgages, deeds, financial statements, tax records, etc.).  
  - The claimant must demonstrate active utilization of the territory for industrial, residential, commercial, or other productive purposes.  
  - Any portion of the claimed territory that has had no title or ownership assigned in the past fifty (50) years at the date of application shall be deemed invalid for that claimant.  
  - In cases of overlapping counter-claims, the last substantiated claim shall prevail.  
  - If no valid claim can be substantiated, the territory defaults to the property of the Hudson Republic.  
- **Miscellaneous Considerations (10–20%)**:  
  - If a validated claim fragments a larger region into non-contiguous or impractical subunits, affected territories shall negotiate to achieve the best contiguous fit.  
  - If continuity cannot be achieved due to intervening validated claims, the applicant may accept the adjusted territory or forfeit the application. This decision is final for the current application.

**4.3 Claim Hierarchy for Overlapping Applications**  
- Overlapping claims submitted for Satellite State status must be resolved by mutual agreement among the affected claimants. The Hudson Republic does not intervene in or adjudicate internal pacts.  
- In cases involving a mix of claim types, priority shall be given to the joining or “orphan” region.
