## Definitions
All capitalized terms used in this Act shall be interpreted in accordance with their definitions in the referenced instruments below.

- **Official Citizen Referendum**: A formal, binding vote of the Citizens on a clearly defined question, conducted in accordance with this Act.  
- **Scouting Referendum**: An informal, non-binding poll or consultation used to gauge public interest or refine a question prior to a formal petition.  
- **SMART Principle**: A question is considered clear if it is Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound.  
- **Citizen Initiative**: A process by which Citizens may propose a referendum question.

## Preamble
The Hudson Republic affirms the sovereign right of its Citizens to directly participate in governance through referendums. This Act establishes clear, transparent, and enforceable standards for both binding Official Citizen Referendums and informal Scouting Referendums. It balances direct democracy with institutional stability while protecting the rights of the Individual.

---

## Section 1 — Official Citizen Referendum

An Official Citizen Referendum is legally binding and must satisfy the following requirements:

**1.1 Petition Threshold**  
To be considered valid for a binding referendum, a petition must be signed by at least **5%** of the registered voters in the affected jurisdiction within the designated petition period.

**1.2 Clarity and Significance**  
The referendum question must:  
- Be clear, neutral, and conform to the **SMART Principle**.  
- Include a concise statement explaining its significance and intended effect.

**1.3 Threshold Determination**  
Upon validation of a qualifying petition, the appropriate official(s) of the affected jurisdiction shall assign the voting threshold required for passage in accordance with the **[Clarity Act](./CA_CLARITYACT_20260522.md)**.

**1.4 Electoral Compliance**  
All Official Citizen Referendums must fully comply with the procedural, security, and integrity requirements of the **[Elections Act](./CA_ELECTIONS_20260401.md)**.

**1.5 Defined Timeline**  
Every Official Citizen Referendum must have clearly published start and end dates for both the petition collection period and the voting period.

---

## Section 2 — Scouting Referendum

**2.1 Purpose**  
A Scouting Referendum is an informal, non-binding consultation designed to measure public interest, test question wording, and build support before launching a formal petition.

**2.2 Nature**  
Scouting Referendums may range from simple anonymous polls to structured mock votes. They carry no legal force and are encouraged as a responsible preliminary step.

---

## Section 3 — Scope of Referendums

Referendums may be held on any matter of public concern brought forward through citizen initiatives, provided they respect the rights guaranteed under **[The Individual (Sovereign) Act](./CA_THEINDIVIDUAL_20260401.md)** and fall within the jurisdictional authority of the Republic or its Ridings.

---

## Section 4 — General Provisions

**4.1 Binding Effect**  
Only Official Citizen Referendums conducted in full compliance with this Act shall be legally binding upon the relevant jurisdiction.

**4.2 Protection of Rights**  
No referendum question or outcome may infringe upon the core rights of the Individual as protected by the Constitution.

---

## Section EX1 — Significance Examples (Non-binding)

**EX1.1 Succession / Independence Question**  
A question regarding secession or succession from the Republic is considered of the highest significance. It requires an **Overwhelming Majority** as defined in the **[Clarity Act](./CA_CLARITYACT_20260522.md)** to become legally binding.

**EX1.2 Local Healthcare Funding in Toronto**  
A question regarding the expansion of healthcare funding within the City of Toronto is of local significance. It requires only a **Simple Majority** as defined in the **[Clarity Act](./CA_CLARITYACT_20260522.md)** and involves only registered voters residing within the affected jurisdiction (Toronto).
