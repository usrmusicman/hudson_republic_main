## Definitions

- **Riding**: A primary territorial and electoral division of the Hudson Republic, defined by land area rather than population, with each Riding approximating 30,000 to 50,000 square kilometers.  
- **Array**: A contiguous block of exactly twelve (12) Ridings, the minimum territorial unit for secession or full accession.  
- **Subzone**: A non-political administrative subdivision within a Riding used solely for logistics, postal, utility, emergency services, or internal mapping purposes.  
- **Satellite State**: A region that maintains a loose, non-voting affiliation with the Hudson Republic.

---

## Preamble

The Hudson Republic is constituted as a single, indivisible sovereign entity whose territory is organized into equal-area Ridings to ensure balanced geographic representation, local accountability, and the rejection of population-weighted or hierarchical governance layers. Sovereignty resides with the People, expressed through direct Riding-based voting and strict constitutional limits on central power in accordance with **[The Individual (Sovereign) Act](./CA_THEINDIVIDUAL_20260401.md)**.

This Act defines the fundamental territorial structure of the Republic, including the concepts of Ridings, Arrays, Subzones, and Satellite States, while absolutely prohibiting intermediate provincial or state layers.

## Section 1 — The Republic and Territorial Basis

**1.1 Indivisible Sovereignty**  
Sovereignty is indivisible and vests in the People as expressed through the Constitution.

**1.2 Territorial Equality**  
Each Riding elects exactly one Representative. Representation is strictly one person, one vote. Riding boundaries are drawn to achieve approximately equal land area.

**1.3 Administrative Subzones**  
Ridings may be divided into Subzones for operational purposes only. Subzones hold **no** political or legislative authority.

---

## Section 2 — Definition and Establishment of Ridings

**2.1 Grid Formula**  
Riding boundaries are determined solely by landmass.  
**Total Riding Size (km²) = Total Landmass of the Republic (km²) ÷ Total Number of Ridings.**

**2.2 Landmass Limits**  
Each Riding shall encompass between **30,000 km² and 50,000 km²**.

**2.3 Tolerance for Irregular Boundaries**  
Limited flexibility (±1% to ±5%) is permitted to accommodate natural geographic features when formally approved.

**2.4 Multiples Rule**  
The total number of Ridings must always be a multiple of twelve (12).

**2.5 Capacity**  
The Republic shall maintain no fewer than 12 and no more than 144 Ridings.

**2.6 No Intermediate Layer**  
There shall be **no provinces or states**. Governance flows directly from the Riding to the federal level.

---

## Section 3 — Mapping and Periodicity of Redraw

**3.1 Ten-Year Lock**  
Riding boundaries are reviewed only once every ten years.

---

## Section 4 — Property and Rights Supremacy

Real property rights are vested at the Riding level. In any conflict between local regulation and **[The Individual (Sovereign) Act](./CA_THEINDIVIDUAL_20260401.md)**, the rights of the Individual prevail absolutely.
