## Definitions

**Simple Majority** — A vote or verdict of greater than 51% in the House of Constituents or 6/12 in the Senate or judicial courts (12-person jury model). Acceptable for routine matters but remains contestable due to its narrow margin.

**Clear Majority / Clear Verdict** — A vote or verdict of 60% or greater in the House of Constituents or 7/12 in the Senate or judicial courts. This constitutes the baseline standard for ordinary legislation and civil judicial matters.

**Overwhelming Majority / Overwhelming Verdict** — A vote or verdict of 75% or greater in the House of Constituents or 9/12 in the Senate or judicial courts. This level conveys a strong and unmistakable mandate.

**Unanimous Vote / Unanimous Verdict** — A vote or verdict of 100% or greater in the House of Constituents or 12/12 in the Senate or judicial courts. This highest standard is reserved for bedrock constitutional articles that are sacred and core to the functioning of the Republic and for all criminal proceedings.

**Registered Representation** — The principle that the validity of any vote, verdict, or referendum requires participation (turnout or presence) proportionate to the applicable majority threshold among registered or seated members.

## Preamble

The Clarity Act establishes objective, quantitative thresholds for decision-making across the legislative and judicial institutions of the Hudson Republic. By defining precise standards of consensus and mandating proportionate participation, this Act eliminates ambiguity, strengthens democratic legitimacy, safeguards individual rights, and ensures that decisions—particularly those of constitutional weight—reflect broad and genuine representation. Constitutional articles requiring unanimous approval are designated as bedrock provisions essential to the Republic’s foundational legal order. The Senate, as the constitutional jury of the Republic, demands full attendance to fulfill its critical role.

---

## Section 1 — Application and Scope

This Act applies to all formal votes in the House of Constituents, all verdicts rendered by the Senate or judicial courts operating under the 12-person jury model, and to referendums conducted under the authority of the Republic or its Ridings.

---

## Section 2 — Voting and Verdict Thresholds

**2.1 House of Constituents**  
- Simple Majority: greater than 51%  
- Clear Majority: 60% or greater  
- Overwhelming Majority: 75% or greater  
- Unanimous: 100% or greater  

**2.2 Senate and Judicial Courts (12-jury model)**  
- Balance of Probabilities: 6/12  
- Clear Verdict: 7/12  
- Overwhelming Verdict: 9/12  
- Unanimous Verdict: 12/12  

---

## Section 3 — Registered Representation Requirement

**3.1** For any vote in the House of Constituents or verdict in the judicial courts to be valid and meet its required threshold, the participation of registered or seated members must achieve a rate at least equal to the applicable majority threshold.  

**3.2** In the Senate, all senators must be present for any vote or proceeding. The full complement of twelve senators constitutes the mandatory quorum at all times, reflecting the Senate’s indispensable role as the constitutional jury of the Republic.

**3.3** Failure to satisfy the Registered Representation requirement renders the vote, verdict, or referendum invalid, irrespective of the margin among those who participated. This ensures that outcomes reflect sufficiently broad and legitimate consensus.

---

## Section 4 — Application of Thresholds

**4.1** Simple Majority (or Balance of Probabilities) shall be used for routine internal matters including house ordinances, administrative rules, and operational standards.  

**4.2** Clear Majority (or Clear Verdict) is the minimum standard required to pass any legislative instrument subject to formal readings.  

**4.3** Overwhelming Majority (or Overwhelming Verdict) is required for foundational constitutional articles and for the formal entry or exit of riding blocks into or from the Republic’s union.  

**4.4** Unanimous Vote (or Unanimous Verdict) is required exclusively for bedrock constitutional articles sacred and core to the Republic’s legal order and for all criminal proceedings to protect the rights of the accused and the integrity of the justice system.
