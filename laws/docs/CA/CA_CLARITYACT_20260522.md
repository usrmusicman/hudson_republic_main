## Definitions

- **Simple Majority / Balance of Probabilities** — A vote of 51% or greater in the House of Constituents, or a verdict of at least 6/12 in the Senate or judicial courts (12-person jury model). Acceptable for routine matters but remains contestable due to its narrow margin.  
- **Clear Majority / Clear Verdict** — A vote of 60% or greater in the House of Constituents, or a verdict of at least 7/12 in the Senate or judicial courts. This is the baseline standard for ordinary legislation and civil judicial matters.  
- **Overwhelming Majority / Overwhelming Verdict** — A vote of 75% or greater in the House of Constituents, or a verdict of at least 9/12 in the Senate or judicial courts. This level conveys a strong and unmistakable mandate.  
- **Unanimous Vote / Unanimous Verdict** — A vote of 100% in the House of Constituents, or 12/12 in the Senate or judicial courts. This highest standard is reserved for bedrock constitutional articles and for all criminal proceedings.  
- **Registered Representation** — The principle that the validity of any vote, verdict, or referendum requires participation (turnout or presence) proportionate to the applicable majority threshold among registered or seated members.

## Preamble

The Clarity Act establishes objective, quantitative thresholds for decision-making across the legislative and judicial institutions of the Hudson Republic. By defining precise standards of consensus and mandating proportionate participation, this Act eliminates ambiguity, strengthens democratic legitimacy, safeguards individual rights, and ensures that decisions — particularly those of constitutional weight — reflect broad and genuine representation.

---

## Section 1 — Application and Scope

This Act applies to all formal votes in the House of Constituents, all verdicts rendered by the Senate or judicial courts (using the 12-person jury model), and to referendums conducted under the authority of the Republic or its Ridings.

---

## Section 2 — Voting and Verdict Thresholds

**2.1 House of Constituents**  
- Simple Majority: 51% or greater  
- Clear Majority: 60% or greater  
- Overwhelming Majority: 75% or greater  
- Unanimous: 100%  

**2.2 Senate and Judicial Courts (12-person jury model)**  
- Balance of Probabilities: at least 6/12  
- Clear Verdict: at least 7/12  
- Overwhelming Verdict: at least 9/12  
- Unanimous Verdict: 12/12  

---

## Section 3 — Registered Representation Requirement

**3.1** For any vote in the House of Constituents or verdict in the judicial courts to be valid, the participation of registered or seated members must achieve a rate at least equal to the applicable majority threshold.  

**3.2** In the Senate, full attendance of all twelve senators is required for any vote or proceeding.  

**3.3** Failure to satisfy the Registered Representation requirement renders the vote, verdict, or referendum invalid, regardless of the margin among those who participated.

---

## Section 4 — Application of Thresholds

**4.1** Simple Majority (Balance of Probabilities) shall be used for routine internal matters, house ordinances, and administrative rules.  

**4.2** Clear Majority (Clear Verdict) is the minimum standard required to pass any legislative instrument subject to formal readings.  

**4.3** Overwhelming Majority (Overwhelming Verdict) is required for foundational constitutional articles and for the formal entry or exit of riding blocks.  

**4.4** Unanimous Vote (Unanimous Verdict) is required exclusively for bedrock constitutional articles and for all criminal proceedings.

## Section EX1 — Illustrative Examples (Non-binding)

**EX1.1** Simple Majority — Routine house rules and internal administrative decisions.

**EX1.3** Overwhelming Majority — Foundational constitutional articles and riding block accession or secession.
