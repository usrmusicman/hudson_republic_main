## Definitions
* **ICR**: Intelligent Character Recognition. Used to convert handwritten text from the ballot capture zone into machine-readable data.
* **Timing Track**: Vertical registration bars located at 0.500" and 7.750" to sync the scanner feed.

---

## Preamble
This legislative instrument provides the layout and calibration specifications for the Hudson Republic Special Accessibility Bi-Election Ballot, configured for single-role contests using high-resolution ICR zones.

---

## Section 1 — ICR Capture Zones
**Page Geometry**: 8.500" × 14.000" (Legal), Portrait, Single-sided.
**Write-In Zone**: 6.500" wide × 2.000" tall (generous space for clear handwriting).
